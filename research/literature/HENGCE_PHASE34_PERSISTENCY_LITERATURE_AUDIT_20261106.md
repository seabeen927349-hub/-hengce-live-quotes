## Crucial ex post vs ex ante distinction
2023 publisher introduction reports a **9.63% first post-formation month** winner-minus-loser spread conditioned on which stocks persist in the *next* month's rank group. Next-month membership is not observable at the initial formation month: **do not market that 9.63% as an executable ex ante signal**. This does NOT accuse the original authors of hindsight bias: the introduction separately reports an **actual persistent long-short strategy** with a **six-month** holding period and **0.756% per month**, ~48 basis points per month above baseline in their historical design. Exact 2023 entry/skip/cost/risk-table details require full paper, so do not certify executable performance or transplant to HENGCE 20-day long-only Top5.

2018 primary PDF https://ah.lib.nccu.edu.tw/bitstream/140.119/113114/1/12140.pdf Section 4.1/Table 5 explicitly defines persistent winners as present at BOTH t−1 and t, and trades only after t, skipping one month, then holding a zero-investment **long winner / short loser** for six months.
