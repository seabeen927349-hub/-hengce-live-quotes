## Evidence scope and findings

`inventory.py` uses `git show` at the fixed commit and reads existing bytes only.
It never imports production code, downloads history, decodes archived research
payloads, generates labels, recomputes rankings, opens holdouts or uses actual
user trades as model evidence. Every consulted file has a Git blob SHA and raw
SHA256 in `evidence_inventory.json`. Embedded observation/decision hashes retain
their original canonicalization; they are not raw-file hashes.

Price rows in the reviewed electronics CSV span 2026-01-02 through 2026-10-22.
PIT membership CSVs span observed sessions in 2023, 2024 and 2025. Row counts and
observed dates do not prove a complete exchange calendar or a complete universe.
EOD manifests cover 2026-10-14, 15, 16, 19, 20, 21 and 22; only 2026-10-21 has
an explicit `formal_complete_day=true` at this pin. Earlier all-leg COMPLETE
statuses are preserved separately from absent formal flags. October 20 and 22
remain incomplete. Repository data does not establish external canonical mother
coverage or complete historical institutional/margin panels.

Q017 has two schema-v5 observation/decision dates, October 21 and 22; E2 has four
postclose states, October 19–22. These are existing outputs, not new baselines
generated by this change. Q017 capture time and computation time are separate.
Fetched/generated timestamps are not evidence of provider publication or first
availability. Git version history is not provider revision history.

M1 has a governance/universe reference but no identified standalone formula,
runner or numerical output in reviewed evidence. Its numerical baseline stays
absent: `electronics_m1` is a market-return field, not M1 engine evidence. Neither
E2 nor full-market shadow can substitute. Supply existing authentic M1 artifacts
in a separately reviewed evidence addition; do not fabricate or reconstruct them.

Daily-next-open research does not inherently need 09:03/09:05 data, but it is
still unauthorized and must meet its own coverage/PIT/cost/execution/governance
gates. Live quote transport/context is not historical executable-volume or fill
evidence. Daily OHLC cannot replace intraday observations.

The existing rolling context snapshot is also inventoried: it contains bid/ask,
cumulative volume and fetch/trade timestamps, but its trade-fresh count is zero
at this pin. It does not establish a historical fill or executable-volume panel.
The weekend transport smoke receipt and Q012 book-integrity contract are retained
as infrastructure/specification evidence, not proof of available raw depth data.

## Governance and approval requirements

`governance.json` freezes no new calibration. E2's existing roundtrip 0.008,
single-slot/no-overlap, next-open and H20 semantics remain the source assumptions.
Brokerage, tax, spread, slippage, participation, lot/tick, latency/fill and research
notional values remain null where not verified/approved. Do not double-count new
components on top of the aggregate frozen E2 cost.

Human policy approval is still needed for component rules/calibration, research
notional/sizing, split boundaries, horizons, purge/embargo, minimum observations
and trades, trial budgets/corrections, drawdown/tail/cost/turnover gates, and
prospective custody/access policy. Any lock-violating subtask must be recorded
`BLOCKED_REQUIRES_EXPLICIT_HUMAN_AUTHORIZATION`; none was executed here. Already
exposed historical dates must not be relabeled pristine.

The ledger validator checks record fields, causal timestamps, separate engines,
zero authority, earlier parents, failed-experiment retention, exact prefixes and
hash links against a caller-provided independent checkpoint. A hash chain by
itself is not immutable: complete rewrites/truncation require external trusted
head/count anchors to detect. No writer, storage service, real experiment ledger,
holdout allocation or data-access mechanism is enabled. JSON Schemas specify
metadata; they do not prove access control, cross-record ordering or atomicity.
Future approved custody must implement single-writer conditional append, durable
independent audit/WORM storage and one-time access before releasing any data.
OPENED is already exposed; interruption cannot restore SEALED status.
